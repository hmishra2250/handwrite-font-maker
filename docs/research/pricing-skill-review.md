## How the skill changed the recommendation

| Skill principle | Applied product decision |
|---|---|
| Read business context before interviewing | Used the repository, working beta and owner’s delegated decisions. Unknown conversion, willingness to pay and cloud costs remain explicitly unknown. |
| Separate package, metric and amount | Package a usable export plus corrections; charge by font project; independently test the dollar amount. |
| Start from value and alternatives | Compare the personal-font outcome and creative workflow, not merely model tokens. Costs constrain viability; competitors do not dictate an identical price. |
| Match monetization to usage | One-off and packs first; recurring billing only for observed repeat creators. |
| Treat initial pricing as a learning bet | $12 is a testable starting recommendation, not an “optimal” price. |
| Research willingness to pay and actual behavior | Use segmented interviews and price research, then real purchases, contribution and repeat use. |
| Make offers understandable | Plain-language limits, real totals, no surprise overages or withheld quality fixes. |

**Deliberate overrides:** generic advice to avoid all low prices, start at $50, assume a particular value-to-price ratio, always offer three tiers, or immediately sell annual subscriptions does not establish what an episodic consumer product should do. Survey sample-size heuristics do not guarantee statistical reliability. No unsupported claim about discount buyers, churn or willingness to pay is imported into this plan.
